Race through 10 rounds as archaeologists decoding a stone wall.

Each round shows five symbols. Find them on your wall and click them in the same order.

Selecting the wrong symbol will clear your current selection and you will have to start that sequence again.

When all five are correct, press Decipher Symbols to reveal the hidden meaning and wait for the next inscription. Faster correct submissions earn more glory.
